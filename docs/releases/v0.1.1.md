## Summary

The first release of Deduparr, a web app that finds the movies and episodes Plex has in more than one copy. It scores every copy on quality, keeps the best one and deletes the others from Radarr or Sonarr, qBittorrent and the disk, with a dry run to try it first.

## Enhancements
• **Setup wizard**: sign in with Plex and connect Radarr, Sonarr and qBittorrent
• **Scans**: pick Plex libraries and find movies and episodes with extra copies
• **Quality scores**: the best copy of each title is chosen to keep
• **Dry run**: try a deletion first without removing anything
• **Deletion**: removes the other copies from Radarr or Sonarr, qBittorrent and disk
• **Hardlinks**: hardlinked copies and samples are not counted as duplicates
• **Dashboard**: duplicates found, space to reclaim and recent deletions at a glance
• **Settings**: change your Plex server and service connections at any time
