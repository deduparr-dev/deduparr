## Summary

This release adds an optional deep scan that also searches your library folders on disk, though for now it only reports what it finds in the logs. Scans also catch identical copies kept in different folders, and Cancel in Settings now really discards your changes.

## Bug fixes
• **Scans**: identical copies in different folders now show up as duplicates
• **Settings**: Cancel now really discards your unsaved changes

## Enhancements
• **Deep scan**: an optional disk search that reports its finds in the logs
