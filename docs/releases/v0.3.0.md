## Summary

You can now choose which copies of a duplicate to keep, and a new scan brings existing duplicates up to date with copies added or removed outside Deduparr.

## Breaking
• **API scripts**: read `sets_updated` and `sets_removed` from a scan instead of `sets_already_exist`

## Enhancements
• **Scan results**: pick which copies to keep and which to delete
• **Rescans**: keep each duplicate's copies in step with your library
