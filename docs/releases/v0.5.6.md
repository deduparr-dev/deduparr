## Summary

Deduparr can now keep the copy that Radarr or Sonarr ranks highest, an option in Settings that is off by default. Scans also pick the right copy to keep when copies were added or removed since the last scan, and the email after a scheduled scan now counts the new duplicate sets.

## Bug fixes
• **Scans**: a title that gets another copy now keeps only the best one
• **Scans**: a title whose kept copy disappears now keeps the next best one
• **Space to reclaim**: copies removed outside Deduparr no longer count
• **Scheduled scans**: the email no longer always reports zero new duplicate sets

## Enhancements
• **Quality profiles**: optionally keep the copy Radarr or Sonarr ranks highest
• **Scan results**: when ranked by Radarr or Sonarr, each copy shows its quality
